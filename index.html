GSTExpanse Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

1. About this policy

GSTExpanse is a business-management application developed by PrinStarc. It provides tools that may include invoicing, billing, inventory, customer and supplier records, reports, tax-related calculations, and AI-powered business assistance. This policy explains how information is handled when you use GSTExpanse.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use and the app configuration, GSTExpanse may process:

  • Account information: details used to create or sign in to an account, such as an email address, display name, and authentication identifiers.
  • Business profile information: business name, business type, country, currency, tax settings, and related preferences you enter.
  • Business records: products, stock, invoices, receipts, customer or supplier details, payment status, and reports that you choose to enter or generate.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting the developer for help.
  • Technical information: app version, device and diagnostic information, and crash or performance data where collected by the app or its enabled SDKs.
  • AI prompts and attachments: text, images, or files you choose to submit to an AI feature, where that feature is enabled.

Please do not enter information that is not needed for your use of the app. You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate basis to enter another person's information into your business records.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide and maintain app features, create invoices and reports, manage business records, synchronize data across devices where synchronization is enabled, authenticate accounts, manage subscriptions, respond to support requests, protect the service, diagnose errors, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Cloud and third-party services

GSTExpanse may use Google Firebase services, such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, or related services, if enabled in the released version. These services may process account, business, or technical information to provide the relevant functionality. Their use is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy information.

GSTExpanse may also rely on other service providers for app distribution, payment processing, analytics, crash reporting, hosting, or AI functionality. The developer should list all providers and SDKs actually included in the released app and describe the information each receives.

5. AI-powered features

If AI-powered features are available, the prompts and content you submit may be sent to the configured AI service provider to generate a response. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information unless the feature explicitly requires it and you are comfortable with the applicable processing. The specific AI provider and its data handling terms should be identified here before release.

6. Advertising

If Google AdMob or another advertising SDK is enabled, the free version of GSTExpanse may display ads. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction, and other information as described in their own disclosures and settings. GSTExpanse is intended to suppress ads for users whose paid subscription entitlement is active. The developer must ensure that the app's actual ad configuration and Google Play Data safety answers match this behavior.

7. Subscriptions and payments

Where paid plans are offered through Google Play, Google Play handles the purchase transaction. GSTExpanse may receive subscription and entitlement information needed to activate or manage paid features. The app does not need to receive your full payment-card number to verify a Google Play purchase. Google's payment and privacy terms also apply.

8. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the features you use, provide support, protect the app, process purchases, or meet legal requirements. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect users, the developer, or the service. PrinStarc does not sell personal information.

9. Storage, security, and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the services and data involved. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the data type and the services enabled.

10. Your choices and data deletion

You may contact us to ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of GSTExpanse. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and accounting purposes. If the app provides an in-app account deletion option, use that option to submit a deletion request. A request by email should include enough information for us to locate the relevant account, but do not send passwords or payment-card details.

11. Children's privacy

GSTExpanse is a business-management tool and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can review the request.

12. International users

Depending on your location and the services enabled, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for such processing.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
