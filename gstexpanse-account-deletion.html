GSTExpanse Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your GSTExpanse account and associated personal data.

How to submit a deletion request

  1. Send an email to prinstarc.official@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line GSTExpanse account deletion request.
  3. Send the request from the email address associated with your GSTExpanse account. If you cannot access that address, explain this in your message so we can guide you through an appropriate verification process.
  4. Include your GSTExpanse account email address and state that you want your account and associated data deleted.

What happens next

We will review the request and may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that you control the account. After verification, we will process the deletion of account-associated personal data, subject to applicable legal, tax, accounting, security, and fraud-prevention obligations.

Data that may need to be retained

Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping obligations. If any data must be retained, it will be limited to what is required and handled according to applicable requirements.

Need help?

For questions about account or data deletion, contact prinstarc.official@gmail.com.

Important: This page provides a way to submit a deletion request. Make sure your support process is monitored and that your app/backend can actually carry out the deletion requests before submitting the Data safety declaration.
